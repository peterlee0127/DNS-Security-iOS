How DNS Security protects you

Every time you open a website or an app connects to a server, your device first asks a DNS resolver for its address. Normal DNS sends these questions in plain text, so anyone on the network, including public Wi-Fi operators and internet providers, can read or change them.

DNS Security configures Apple's system encrypted DNS so these lookups are sent over DNS over HTTPS or DNS over TLS to the provider you choose. Only DNS is affected, so your speed and your other traffic stay exactly as they were.

  1. Choose a DNS profilePick a built-in provider in the Config tab or add your own.
  2. Turn it onEnable the switch in the Connect tab.
  3. Confirm in SettingsGo to General › VPN & Network › DNS and select DNS Security.
